Photosensitivity note

Bolometer adds no flashes of its own: the shutter freezes the picture, it doesn't flash it. But its auto-ranging stretches whatever temperatures the clip has across the whole palette, so a dark clip's small flicker can become a large flash. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.

Measured over eight of Resolume's demo clips at the plugin's defaults (9 Hz, Ironbow), it stayed at three flashes a second or fewer; at 60 Hz, or with Rainbow, two of them went over, up to 4.5 a second over a third of the picture. This page opens on the defaults over the harness's card, and that view, measured the same way from the page's own output for 40 seconds through the first shutter, made no flash across the picture and one in 40 seconds in the worst third of it. The same view at 60 Hz measured the same; with Rainbow, and with each preset over 12 seconds, it made none. A clip of your own is stretched the same way. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
